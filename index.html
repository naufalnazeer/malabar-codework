Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do you only work with clients in Kerala?

Yes — currently we take on projects only for shops, businesses, creators and startups based in Kerala. This lets us offer faster turnaround and, where useful, in-person meetings.

Is hosting really free?

Yes. We host your website at no cost to you, for as long as it's active. The only recurring cost is your domain name, which you pay directly to the domain registrar — typically ₹700–₹1,500 per year.

Will my app really be free to publish on the Play Store?

Yes. We publish your app to Google Play under our existing developer account, so you don't pay the standard one-time Google Play registration fee. If you'd prefer the app listed under your own developer account later, that can be arranged separately.

How long does a project take?

Starter websites: roughly 5–10 days. E-commerce stores: 2–4 weeks. Mobile apps: 3–6 weeks. Booking platforms: 4–8 weeks, depending on complexity.

What's the payment structure?

Typically 50% advance to begin work and 50% on delivery. Larger projects can be split into milestone-based payments instead.

Do you provide support after launch?

Every project includes a free support window after launch to fix any issues. After that, optional AMC (annual maintenance) plans are available for content updates and ongoing changes.

Can you redesign or migrate my existing website?

Yes — we handle redesigns, platform migrations, and speed/SEO improvements for existing websites too.

Is the YouTube channel and monetization guidance really free?

Yes. We share this over a WhatsApp chat or quick call at no cost — covering channel setup, branding, upload strategy, and the eligibility requirements for monetization. No purchase required.

Do you manage Facebook/Instagram ads, or just set them up?

Both — we set up your Business account, Meta Business Suite, and ad campaigns, and can manage them ongoing. Note that ad spend (the budget paid to Meta for running the ads) is separate from our setup/management fee.
